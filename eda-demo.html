Quality review

EDA asset review checklist

This review structure helps an AI research workflow evaluate whether an electronics design asset is understandable, simulated, documented, and ready for deeper engineering review.

Schematic and simulation checks

  • Component references and values are visible and consistent.
  • Units are documented for resistance, capacitance, voltage, and frequency.
  • SPICE netlist matches the schematic topology.
  • Analysis type and frequency sweep are explicitly listed.
  • Cutoff frequency and time constant are calculated from the component values.

PCB and handoff checks

  • Input, output, and ground are labeled.
  • Trace width is documented as a design parameter.
  • Component placement is readable and easy to inspect.
  • Board concept separates schematic intent from layout assumptions.
  • Review notes clearly state that this is a demo asset, not a fabrication release.

How this maps to the survey tools

Relevant EDA tool patterns represented

  • KiCAD: schematic capture, component labeling, PCB layout preview, and board review workflow.
  • LibrePCB: component placement, board concept organization, and layout handoff thinking.
  • Ngspice / LTspice: SPICE-style netlist generation, AC sweep, and circuit response analysis.
  • Qucs-S: simulation setup, parameter-driven component values, and result interpretation.
  • DesignSpark: practical electronics design review, schematic-to-layout reasoning, and export planning.
  • AI research workflow: structured metadata, reproducible analysis, and reviewer-ready notes.

Quality review

BIM / IFC-style review thinking

Even though this sample is a small mechanical part, the same review mindset applies to AEC models: digital assets should have clear classification, units, constraints, metadata, and assumptions.

Geometry checks

  • Named dimensions are visible and traceable.
  • Hole spacing is symmetric around the centerline.
  • Hole diameter does not exceed practical edge clearance.
  • Thickness is represented as a model parameter, not a hidden assumption.

Metadata checks

  • Object name: Parametric Mounting Bracket.
  • Units: millimeters.
  • Model class: mechanical support / mounting component.
  • Review status: demo asset for AI research portfolio.

How this maps to the survey tools

Relevant tool patterns represented

  • FreeCAD / SolveSpace: constraint-based thinking and dimensional design intent.
  • BIM / IFC: review checklist mindset for model classification, metadata, assumptions, and validation.

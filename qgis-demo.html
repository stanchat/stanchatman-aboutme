Quality review

Geospatial asset review checklist

This is the kind of structure that helps an AI research workflow evaluate whether a GIS asset is accurate, documented, and usable.

Spatial checks

  • Coordinate reference system is documented.
  • Units are clearly stated for distance-based analysis.
  • Service area and risk buffers are generated from named parameters.
  • Candidate site scoring uses traceable assumptions.

Data quality checks

  • Source and derived layers are separated.
  • Layer names are readable and purpose-driven.
  • Symbology supports interpretation without hiding risk factors.
  • Processing notes are complete enough for another analyst to reproduce.

How this maps to the survey tools

Relevant QGIS patterns represented

  • Layer management: source layers, derived layers, symbology, and output groups.
  • Vector analysis: parcel review, point features, line features, and polygon overlays.
  • Buffer analysis: service radius and risk buffer logic with documented distance units.
  • Spatial decision support: proximity, risk, and need scoring for a candidate site.
  • AI research workflow: structured metadata, reproducible steps, and reviewer-ready notes.

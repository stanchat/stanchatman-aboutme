Quality review

Digital media asset review checklist

This is the type of review structure that helps an AI research workflow evaluate whether a media asset is usable, consistent, and well documented.

Video checks

  • Timeline has clear video, voice, music, and SFX tracks.
  • Cut pace matches the intended content type and audience.
  • Lower-third text is readable and not covering critical content.
  • Export target is documented for downstream review.

Audio checks

  • Voiceover remains the loudest and clearest element.
  • Music supports the message without overpowering speech.
  • Sound effects are used intentionally and not overmixed.
  • Track names and mix notes are clear enough for handoff.

How this maps to the survey tools

Relevant tool patterns represented

  • Shotcut / OpenShot: timeline editing, clip sequencing, track organization, and export planning.
  • Lightworks: edit decision thinking, pacing, scene selection, and timeline review.
  • LMMS: music bed, effects layering, and simple mix-level control.
  • Ardour: multitrack audio session organization, dialogue/music/SFX separation, and mix QA.
  • AI research workflow: structured metadata, repeatable review criteria, and documented asset decisions.
